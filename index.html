Soundboard

Sounds

Every button has a built-in sound to start with. Tap Choose file to swap in your own (an MP3, M4A, WAV, or even a screen recording saved on this tablet; silence at the start and end is trimmed automatically). Sounds stay on this tablet and work offline.

    Tip: pin the app so little hands can't swipe out of it. In Android Settings, go to Security → App pinning (or "Pin windows"), then pin this app from the Recents screen.
